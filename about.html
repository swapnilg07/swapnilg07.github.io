About Swapnil Gupta

Senior Software Engineer II at KPI Partners, Bengaluru

I’m Swapnil Gupta, a full stack developer based in Bengaluru, India. I have spent 5+ years building web applications with ReactJS, TypeScript, Python and Django, and I currently work as a Senior Software Engineer II at KPI Partners on enterprise data and analytics solutions. Online I go by swapnilg07.

Where I started

I did my Bachelor of Computer Applications at Dr. MPS College of Business Studies, Agra, from 2014 to 2017. In 2018 I secured All India Rank 52 in NIMCET, the national MCA entrance exam, which took me to the National Institute of Technology, Tiruchirappalli (NIT Trichy). I completed my Master of Computer Applications there in 2021.

During the MCA I won first position in THINKLTICS at LOGIN’19, the national level MCA symposium at PSG College, Coimbatore, and interned at Trawello HealthyKID, where I built REST APIs for authentication and user registration in a health tracker web application.

My career so far

I began at HSBC Electronic Data Processing India as an Analyst in March 2021, building monitoring dashboards with Python, Dash and ReactJS and automating internal email newsletters.

In March 2022 I moved to Halliburton Development Center India as a Software Developer. There I developed ReactJS dashboards backed by Python APIs and handled debugging, troubleshooting and L3 production support.

From November 2023 to September 2025 I was an Engineer II at Quoqo Technologies, working on Quoqo Fusion, a legal management platform with e-signature, DSC and Aadhaar signing, AI-powered document review, a built-in editor, Office 365 integration and secure document storage.

I joined KPI Partners in October 2025 as a Senior Software Engineer II.

What I build

Most of my work is full stack: React and TypeScript interfaces on the front, Python and Django services and APIs behind them, deployed on AWS or Azure. More recently I have been building with generative AI, including AI agents, AI API integrations and prompt engineering workflows with Claude, ChatGPT and GitHub Copilot.

Outside of work I build my own products:

  • InvoicePro, a free GST invoice generator for Indian businesses with automatic CGST/SGST/IGST calculation and PDF download.
  • DriveMaster, a web-based file management platform with secure sharing and access control.
  • TemplateEASE, a document workflow application with DOCX editing, OCR-based PDF conversion and AI-assisted review.
